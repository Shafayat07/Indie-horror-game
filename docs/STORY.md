# Story bible — THE TIDE REMEMBERS (short)

**The island.** Vey Isle, North Atlantic. The upper settlement (Vey) was built after the lower village,
**Low Vey**, drowned in the storm of 15 November 1923. The night before, keeper Aldo Wren logged
**thirteen seconds** in which every sound on the island stopped.

**Project Pelagic (1968–71).** Admiralty early-warning research led by **Dr. Ruth Marrow**: listen to the sea
for "pre-acoustic" signals that arrive before their causes. Weather station + a transmitter beneath the
lighthouse. Research vessel **MV Pelagic**. At full gain the transmitter heard the thirteen seconds again —
and **something answered**. The Listener appeared: a tall shape of wet rope, net and ship parts that walks
toward sounds, even sounds that have not happened yet. What it is stays unexplained; Marrow's last note says
it was there before Low Vey drowned.

**Project Morrow.** The medical arm, under **Dr. Edwin Morrow**, in an annex beneath the caves: cochlear
grafts so human volunteers could hear pre-echoes. Twelve subjects; eleven vanished. The crew of the Pelagic
were "collected" for it. **Subject 12, Tomas Kell** (the station's radio operator) adapted: he became a
bat-like man who hunts by sonar, roosts by his old radio tower, and leaves only when the bell strikes the hour.
**Subject 13** is listed as *E. Vale — arrives by sea*.

**People.** Aldo Wren (harbourmaster, lighthouse keeper) · Ruth Marrow (lead scientist) · Tomas Kell (radio
operator, last subject) · Ines Halloran (research assistant; letter to her daughter Nell) · Edwin Morrow
(medical director).

**Elias Vale** is sent to restore the station and transmit its report. The island has heard him coming all
night: his own voice on the radio, his own footsteps ahead of him, his name in Morrow's list.

**Suha.** Elias's partner on the mainland. Her voice reaches him on radios that cannot reach that far — the
island has been listening to him all night and learned the name he says when he is afraid. Her calls are the
island's lure.

**Endings.** Break the signal (silence; the warning is never sent) · Send the warning (the mainland is
warned; the signal spreads) · The Last Recording (play Morrow's tape, the Pelagic mayday and Kell's message
into the transmitter so it hears itself; the Listener stops) · The Light (follow Suha's voice to the lantern room;
she was never there — Elias drowned at 03:12 and becomes the voice that keeps the light).
